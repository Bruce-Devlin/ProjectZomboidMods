title=[B42.20] [SP/MP] Getting Old
description=[h1]Getting Old — Dynamic Aging for Build 42.20[/h1]
description=
description=Surviving the apocalypse for years should leave a mark.
description=
description=Getting Old gives every survivor a real age and date of birth. Birthdays pass, hair greys, life stages change, and the physical cost of age gradually catches up with you. Youth has its advantages. Old age has consequences.
description=
description=[h1]A Life, Not Just a Number[/h1]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Choose your birthday when a new character first enters the world, or let the server randomize it.
description=[*]Start at a random age from 25–34, use an exact sandbox-configured age, or select an age trait during character creation.
description=[*]Age naturally as in-game years pass. The length of a year is fully configurable from 1 to 365 days.
description=[*]See your Age, Life Stage and full Date of Birth in the Health panel.
description=[*]Watch hair and beards progressively turn grey from age 30 onward.
description=[*]Celebrate birthdays with survivor dialogue and an optional chance to receive a personalized party hat.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Five Life Stages[/h1]
description=
description=[b]Zoomer:[/b] 12–17
description=[b]Young:[/b] 18–25
description=[b]Adult:[/b] 26–40
description=[b]Middle-aged:[/b] 41–60
description=[b]Elderly:[/b] 61+
description=
description=Age influences endurance, fatigue, pain and stress. Younger survivors recover more easily, while middle-aged and elderly survivors face increasingly difficult physical effects. Elderly survivors may also stumble and fall.
description=
description=Life-stage changes can remove traits that no longer fit and award a new age-appropriate trait, allowing a long-lived character to change with time rather than remaining frozen at character creation.
description=
description=[h1]Character-Creation Age Traits[/h1]
description=
description=Optional, mutually exclusive traits let you begin at a specific age:
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Age: Zoomer[/b] — start at 12
description=[*][b]Age: Young[/b] — start at 21
description=[*][b]Age: Adult[/b] — start at 30
description=[*][b]Age: Middle aged[/b] — start at 45
description=[*][b]Age: Elderly[/b] — start at 70
description=[/list]
description=
description=If no age trait is selected, the sandbox starting-age settings are used instead.
description=
description=[h1]Old Age Can Be Fatal[/h1]
description=
description=By default, survivors who reach the Deadly Age are marked for death from old age. Their overall health then declines gradually over a configurable number of years, with each survivor receiving a slightly different lifespan. Immersive warnings appear as the end approaches.
description=
description=Old-age death can be disabled completely by setting [b]Deadly Age[/b] to 0.
description=
description=For an extra risk, server owners can enable [b]Deadly Age Heart Attacks[/b]. Survivors at or above the deadly age may suffer a fatal heart attack while extremely stressed or panicked, with the risk rising sharply with age and danger. This option is [b]disabled by default[/b].
description=
description=[h1]Sandbox Options[/h1]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Random or exact starting age
description=[*]Player-selected or randomized birthdays
description=[*]Year length: 1–365 in-game days
description=[*]Deadly age: 0–120 (0 disables old-age death)
description=[*]Years until death: 1–20, with ±10% variation per survivor
description=[*]Optional deadly-age heart attacks
description=[*]Optional birthday hats
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Project Zomboid Build 42.20
description=[*]Singleplayer and multiplayer
description=[*]Can be added to an existing save, although a new character is recommended
description=[*]Age and birthday data persist with the character
description=[*]Does not change zombie behaviour
description=[/list]
